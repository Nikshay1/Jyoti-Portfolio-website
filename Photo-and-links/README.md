# Photo and links

Drop Jyoti's material here, then ask Claude to wire it into the site.

- **Photo**: any `.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`. A portrait with plain space around it works best; the site shows it in black and white and fades to colour on hover.
- **Links**: put them in `links.md`, one per line, as `Title of the piece — https://...`. Live articles, landing pages, and sites she wrote. Mark the one you most want people to see.
